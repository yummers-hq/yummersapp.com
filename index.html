Been there?
Say so.

Tap Liked it or Didn’t like it. No scores to agonise over. Add what you ordered and who you went with if you want to.

Lists for
every mood.

Save a place to Want to try, Been here or Favourites, or make lists of your own. Coming from Google Maps? Paste a list’s link and bring your places over.

Your Google Maps saves, finally useful.

  1. Share the list in Google Maps

    Open the list, tap Share, let anyone with the link view it, and copy the link.

  2. Paste the link in yummers

    Lists, +, Import a list, Paste the link, then Find places.

  3. Pick the places, tap Import

    The sure matches are already ticked.
