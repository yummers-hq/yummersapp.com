No iPhone? Join the Android list

  1. Open the link on your iPhone

    Tap Join on iPhone on your phone. TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying apps before they're in the App Store.

  2. Get TestFlight

    If you don't have it yet, the link takes you to TestFlight in the App Store. Tap Get, then come back to this page and tap the link again. You're already signed in with your Apple ID, so there's nothing to fill in.

  3. Install yummers

    Tap Accept, then Install. yummers appears on your home screen like any other app. Sign in with Apple, Google or your email.

What to try first

  • Rate a place you've eaten. A green thumb or a red one, and a few words if you like.
  • Make a list and share it with a friend, or bring one over from Google Maps.
  • Follow a friend and see what they've rated.

Tell us what you think

Take a screenshot in yummers and TestFlight asks if you'd like to send it to us with a note. Or email hello@yummersapp.com. We read everything.

Everything you rate and save stays when yummers comes to the App Store.
