yummers is a social food diary for Cambodia: rate places, keep lists and share them with friends.

Contact us

Email

The best way to reach yummers is by email. Write to hello@yummersapp.com for anything: questions about the app, feedback and ideas, partnerships, press, or a restaurant you think we should know about.

Privacy and deleting your account

For questions about your information, or to ask for a copy of it, email privacy@yummersapp.com. You can delete your account yourself in the app, under Profile. If you can't get into the app, see how to delete your account by email.

Help

Moving a list over from Google Maps? See how to import your Google Maps lists.

Restaurants

If you run a restaurant and its name, address, opening hours or photos are wrong in the app, email hello@yummersapp.com with what should change and we'll fix it.

Reporting a problem

To report a person, a review or a list, use the report option inside the app, so we can see exactly what you mean. If you think we made the wrong call on a report or on your account, email hello@yummersapp.com and we'll reply within 14 days.

Social media

You can also find us on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn.
